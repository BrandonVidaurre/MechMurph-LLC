Small-engine repair, done right

Mowers, trimmers, edgers, blowers, pressure washers — if it runs on a small engine, Murph can fix it. Won't start, won't stay running, cuts uneven, leaking oil, pull cord stuck: bring it by the bench and we'll tell you what's actually wrong.

Every machine gets a straight diagnosis and a clear price up front. We don't work on commission and we don't pad the ticket. If a repair isn't worth the money, we'll say so and point you the right way.

  • Won't-start diagnosis
  • Carburetor clean / rebuild
  • Full tune-up (oil, plug, air filter, blade)
  • Blade sharpening & balancing
  • Pull cord / recoil replacement
  • Deck, belt & drive repair
  • Self-propel / other services
