Meet Murph

Murph has been elbow‑deep in small engines most of his life, and MechMurph is the shop that grew out of it — a straightforward, hard‑working operation right here in San Antonio. The same person who diagnoses your mower is the one who fixes it and hands it back running right.

Jason brings more than 20 years of diesel experience from his time in the Army, plus hands‑on work on oil rigs and commercial diesel fleets. He’s spent years as a humble mobile mechanic serving neighborhoods all over San Antonio — Alamo Heights, Stone Oak, Southtown — and when he’s off the clock, he’s usually under the hood of a car just for the fun of it.
